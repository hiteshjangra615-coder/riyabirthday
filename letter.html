Tap the envelope. Read this slowly.

Happy 21st Birthday, Riya ❤️

Happy 21st Birthday meri Riya, meri jaan, meri princess ❤️🎂✨

Riya… meri jaan… ❤️
Aaj ka din mere liye bhi bohot zyada special hai, kyunki aaj meri life ke un sabse pyaare insaano mein se ek ka birthday hai, jiske bina shayad meri life itni khoobsurat hoti hi nahi. 🥹❤️

Happy Birthday mere bacha, meri Riya, meri princess, meri rasmalai aur meri sabse pyaari bestie. 🫶🏻❤️

21 saal ki ho gayi meri bachi… 🥹❤️
Pata hi nahi chalta na waqt kab aur kaise itni jaldi nikal jata hai. Kal tak to chhoti si bachi lagti thi, aaj 21 ki ho gayi. 😂❤️
Lekin mere liye tum hamesha meri wahi pyaari si bacha rahogi jiske liye dil mein ek alag hi jagah hai. 🥺🫶🏻

Sach bolu Riya, duniya mein bohot log milte hain, bohot log dost bante hain… lekin kuch log seedha dil mein ghar bana lete hain.
Aur meri rasmalai, tum unhi kuch khaas logon mein se ho. ❤️

Tumhari smile, tumhari cute-cute baatein, tumhare nakhre, tumhara gussa, tumhari care… sab kuch mere liye bohot special hai. 🥹❤️
Kabhi-kabhi shayad main words mein bata nahi pata ki tum mere liye kitni important ho, lekin dil se ek baat hamesha yaad rakhna—

Tum bohot zyada special ho, meri jaan. ❤️

Meri princess, main bas ye chahta hoon ki tumhari life mein kabhi kisi cheez ki kami na ho. ✨
Tum hamesha khush raho, hamesha smile karti raho aur tumhari aankhon mein sirf khushi ke aansu aayein. 🥹❤️

Zindagi mein chahe kitne bhi log aayein, kitne bhi saal beet jaayein aur kitni bhi cheezein badal jaayein…
meri dua hamesha tumhare saath rahegi. ❤️

Kabhi tum udaas ho, kabhi sab kuch ajeeb sa lage, ya kabhi lage ki koi samajhne wala nahi hai, toh bas yaad rakhna—
tumhara ye pagal bestie hamesha tumhari side pe khada milega. 🫶🏻❤️

Main tumhari har problem solve kar paun, zaroori nahi…
Lekin tumhari problems mein tumhe akela feel na hone doon, ye zaroor chahta hoon. ❤️

Aur meri bacha, kabhi khud ko kam mat samajhna.
Tum jitni cute aur khoobsurat ho, usse bhi zyada pyaara tumhara dil hai. 🥹❤️
Tum deserve karti ho duniya ki saari happiness, saara sukoon aur woh saari khushiyan jinke baare mein tumne kabhi kisi ko bataya bhi nahi. ✨

Aaj tumhara 21st birthday hai, toh meri taraf se tumhare liye bas ek wish hai—

Tumhari har wish poori ho, tumhara har sapna sach ho, tumhari har subah smile ke saath ho aur tumhari zindagi ka har saal pichhle saal se bhi zyada beautiful ho. ❤️✨

Aur haan meri rasmalai… 😂❤️
Apne nakhre kabhi kam mat karna, apni cute si pagalpan wali harkatein kabhi mat badalna aur sabse important—

apni ye pyaari si smile kabhi mat khona. 🥹❤️

Kyunki tumhari smile dekhkar sach mein dil ko bohot sukoon milta hai. 🫶🏻

Riya, thank you…
Meri life mein aane ke liye, mujhe samajhne ke liye, meri bakwaas sunne ke liye, mere saath hasne ke liye aur meri life ko itni saari beautiful memories dene ke liye. ❤️

Tum sirf meri bestie nahi ho, tum meri life ki un sabse precious memories ka hissa ho jinhe main kabhi khona nahi chahta. 🥹❤️

Toh once again…

HAPPY 21ST BIRTHDAY MERI JAAN, MERI BACHA, MERI PRINCESS, MERI RASMALAI, MERI RIYA ❤️🎂🫶🏻

Happy Birthday Riya… meri jaan. ❤️✨
